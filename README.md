# ArchiSpace

### A personal space for learning, creating and growing.

ArchiSpace brings college life and creative work together in one calm, organised website. Built around **Archit Renjeev’s** journey as an Artificial Intelligence & Data Science student at **Amrita Bengaluru**, it combines academic planning, everyday progress, project ideas and a personal portfolio.

The goal is simple: make it easier to understand where you stand, decide what to work on next and give your ideas room to become something real.

## The academic workspace

### Semester overview

A single view of the current semester, including recorded GPA, course credits, attendance health, degree-credit progress and upcoming tasks. The dashboard connects long-term goals with small, actionable next steps.

### Courses & credits

A semester-wise home for course names, codes, credits, faculty details and private notes. ArchiSpace begins with Archit’s nine first-semester courses, totalling **26 credits**, and supports additional courses and semesters as his studies progress.

### SGPA & CGPA

Credit-weighted calculations show how each course contributes to semester and cumulative performance. Recorded grades stay separate from hypothetical grades, making it possible to explore different outcomes without changing actual results.

Incomplete grade entries are clearly labelled as partial results. Failed grades contribute zero points while remaining in the relevant GPA credit total; blank grades are not mistaken for failures.

### Attendance

Individual course tracking shows attended classes, total classes and attendance percentage. Planning estimates show how many classes can be missed while staying above the minimum, or how many consecutive classes are needed to recover.

The starting attendance minimum is **75% per course**.

### Assessments & marks

An assessment ledger brings together quizzes, mid-semester exams, end-semester exams, labs, projects and participation. Raw marks and their contribution to the final result are treated separately, reflecting the different ways courses are assessed.

Personal marks targets show the performance needed across remaining assessments. Pending marks remain distinct from a genuine score of zero.

### Goal planning

The CGPA planner works backwards from a desired result to show the average needed across future GPA-bearing credits. It identifies mathematically unreachable targets as well as achievable ones.

A task list turns larger ambitions into smaller steps, with optional due dates and course links.

### Timetable & grade history

The timetable keeps the original semester schedule close at hand. Grade-change history and backlog visibility provide context as recorded results evolve.

## Beyond the classroom

### Idea garden

A home for course projects, experiments, questions and personal ideas. Ideas can be linked to a subject and moved through three stages:

- **Seed** — an early thought worth keeping.
- **Growing** — an idea receiving attention and effort.
- **Ready** — something developed enough to take further.

A separate feature wishlist captures ideas for ArchiSpace itself.

### Personal portfolio

A public-facing introduction to Archit, his interests, skills and selected work. Projects can remain private drafts or be marked for inclusion in portfolio exports.

The portfolio focuses on real work and honest descriptions of experience, with space to grow as new projects are completed.

### Résumé

A printable résumé draws from the personal profile and selected public projects. Academic tracking data and private project drafts are excluded from the public presentation.

## Thoughtful academic guidance

ArchiSpace distinguishes recorded results, planning estimates and unconfirmed academic rules.

- Grading references are identified separately from confirmed batch-specific requirements.
- Marks-to-grade estimates depend on explicitly supplied course cutoffs rather than an assumed universal chart.
- FIH is represented as pass/fail; its default exclusion from GPA remains a provisional assumption.
- The **180-credit** degree target is provisional and refers to the whole degree, not an individual subject.
- Passing a course may involve attendance, practicals and other requirements beyond its total marks.

The website is a personal planning companion, not an official university record or a guarantee of academic eligibility.

## A calm, personal design

A forest-green and soft-ivory palette gives ArchiSpace a quiet, focused identity. Clear sections, simple navigation and responsive layouts keep the experience comfortable on both desktop and mobile.

The workspace is designed to feel personal rather than institutional: a place for study, reflection, experimentation and steady progress.

## Privacy & personal ownership

Academic records, attendance and notes are saved in the current browser rather than automatically shared online. Full workspace backups are separate from public portfolio and résumé exports.

Public exports include the profile and explicitly selected projects, not private academic records. The current workspace does not provide a password-protected account or cross-device cloud synchronisation.

## Room to grow

ArchiSpace is intended to evolve alongside its owner, with room for new subjects, projects and personal tools. A personal AI assistant remains a future idea; it is not connected in the current website.
